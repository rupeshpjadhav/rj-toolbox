About My Toolbox

My Toolbox (rj-toolkit.com) is a free collection of everyday utilities for developers, QA engineers and data folks: a test data generator, password and key generator, CSV/TSV/JSON/XML converter, JSON, SQL and YAML formatters and validators, a text difference checker, and case converters.

How the tools work

The tools run in your web browser. The text and files you paste or upload are processed on your device and are not stored on our server.
